# Mistakes We've Made and what We've Learnt

## Updating Firmware later on
Currently, we are having to deal with the pheonix pro licensing stuff in week 3, instead of having dealt with the licensing and firmware updates back in week 1.

## Little Practical Newbie Training
The kids did have the core experiences like build, deploying and testing their code, but they have had little experience outside of the main wpilib programs and have little practice with SysID, advantageScope, shuffleboard, and the Limelight dashboard. There are other software like Glass, which we don't use, but still it would be nice to have shown and had them experiment with these apps during the training period. This hasn't fully came back to bite us yet, but will in later years in terms of speed, so let's get them fully caught up and ready to go.

## Not researching new tools
We got Krakens and we're using Phoenix more, so I wish we had read up on TalonFX and the abilities of the motor controllers like motion magic and the features of Phoenix tuner like SwerveDrive testing
