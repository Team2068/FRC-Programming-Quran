Situation:
During this year, we had just come from a nearly successful year. We had won our way to districts last year and started

Weaknesses:
- Slow CADing due to one person CADing. Utsav (mechanical lead at the time) did most of the CADing and with us also doing a prototype robot along with our main robot, we did run late for our build and had little testing and tuning time and had a lot more time fixing wiring issues or mechanical issues, slowing down any usable progress. 
- Not very well trained Newbies. We tried training them to program sensors and they did get some minor experience, but nothing substantial. All were either still learning how to code, so needed to do lessons in their free time or were given tasks that didn't really challenge their abilities and taught them very little.
- Many connectors connecting energy chain (more points of failure which did fail frequently by unplugging)
- Testing and tuning were slow. We constantly had to fix offsets, PID tuning was slow and due to our robot having an arm with no safety limits, left it quite dangerous. We also lacked experience with arm like mechansims, so that did increase the danger factor but as we progressed we found out modeling the intended PID position (though a motion profile) against our current position did help tune velocity and acceleration to ensure we had reasonable time frames along with modeling PID output vs actual for non-profiled also being useful as we can see how much it may over-estimate itself

Strengths:
- We had a lot of new sponsors and a lot of money flowing in which helped produce our robot, but with how our competitions went I feel as if we lost a large part of our trust with their funds seemingly wasted on a bottom tier robot. Something to always think about is how can your robot attract customers/sponsors as if you want to keep building robots, you'll need a lot of money and there any possible chance to convince someone to give it to you.
- We had an almost successful year in 2022, so there was a spark and confidence in our abilities, which eventually turned into a hunger for redemption and success when we failed miseribly.
