- CANCoder issues as well for steer modules. I recommend not using CANCoders (unless they release new hardware and that has more area for their soldering pads and seem to be more reliable) and instead if you have the option to use Falcons, Krakens, or some other motor with an integrated Absolute Encoder, then use that one, it will reduce the wiring needs and the points of failure on your drivetrain which can be a life saver later on.
